What Is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence is a field of computer science that focuses on creating systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Examples include understanding language, recognising images, making predictions, and solving problems.

Applications of AI

Healthcare

AI can help medical professionals analyse data, study medical images, and support research.

Education

AI tools can help students practise concepts and receive personalised learning support.

Transportation

AI can support traffic prediction, route planning, and the development of driver assistance systems.

What Does the Future Hold?

AI may transform scientific research, software development, communication, and everyday life. Responsible development will be important to ensure that its benefits are shared fairly.
